Employee User Guide

Everything you need to know to use CrewOne HR — your company's attendance, leave, and HR management system.

Login

1

Open the system

Go to your company's HR link in the browser.

2

Enter your Employee ID and Password

Your Employee ID and initial password were given to you by your admin when your account was created.

3

Change your password

It is recommended to change your password after first login. Go to Profile → Change Password.

If you forget your password, contact your administrator — they can reset it for you.

Dashboard

Your dashboard is the main page you see after logging in. It shows:

Today's Attendance

Your sign-in and sign-out time, whether you were on time or late.

Task & Break Panel

Log tasks and take breaks while you are marked IN.

Notifications

Company announcements and holiday notices.

Monthly Record

Your attendance summary and downloadable CSV report.

Attendance Heatmap

Visual history of your attendance over the last 364 days.

My Information

Your employee profile (read-only — contact admin to change).

Attendance (IN / OUT)

1

Click the green IN button

When you arrive at work, click the big green IN button on your dashboard. Your sign-in time is recorded instantly.

2

Late arrival — provide a reason

If you arrive after your shift start time (plus grace period), a popup will appear asking for the reason. Type your reason and click Submit, or click Skip.

3

Click the red OUT button when leaving

Always click OUT before you leave. The system does not auto-mark you OUT when you close the browser.

4

Undo OUT (10-minute window)

If you clicked OUT by mistake, you have 10 minutes to undo it using the Undo OUT button.

If you forgot to click OUT the previous day, the system will ask you to confirm your OUT time before you can mark IN again.

Attendance Status

BadgeMeaning
On timeYou arrived within your shift start + grace period.
LateYou arrived after the grace period. Late minutes are shown.
Not markedYou have not marked IN yet today.

Breaks

You can log breaks while you are marked IN. Break categories available:

  • Lunch — Meal break
  • Prayer — Namaz / prayer break
  • Personal — Personal errands
  • Other — Any other reason
1

Select break type and click Start Break

Available from the Task & Break panel on your dashboard (only when you are marked IN).

2

Click End Break when you return

Your break duration is recorded automatically.

Tasks

Log what you are working on throughout the day. Tasks track time automatically.

1

Type your task name and click Start Task

e.g. "Client proposal", "Email follow-ups", "Design review"

2

Click End Task when done

Time spent on the task is saved. You can start a new task immediately.

All today's tasks are shown in a table on your dashboard. Monthly task logs can be downloaded as CSV.

Leave Requests

Apply for leave directly from your dashboard under Apply for leave / off.

1

Select dates and leave type

Choose your start and end date, and select the type (Annual Leave, Sick Leave, etc.).

2

Add a reason (optional)

Briefly explain why you need the leave.

3

Submit and wait for approval

Your admin will approve or reject the request. You will see the status update in My Leave Requests.

Your leave balance (days used vs. total quota) is shown at the top of the leave section.

Calendar

The calendar on your dashboard shows your attendance, approved leaves, and company holidays for each month. You can navigate between months using the arrows.

Notifications

Company announcements, holiday notices, and policy updates are shown in the Notifications section on your dashboard and in the Notifications tab.

You can enable browser push notifications from your profile so you get alerts even when the app is not open.

Company Policy

Your company's policies are listed in the Policy tab. Read all policy points carefully.

1

Read all policy points

Scroll through each policy point. Bold text highlights important rules; bullet lists break down sub-points.

2

Click Acknowledged

At the bottom, click the blue Acknowledged button. It turns green to confirm you have read the policy.

When admin adds or updates a policy, your acknowledgment resets. You will get a notification and must re-read and re-acknowledge.

My Information

At the bottom of your dashboard, the My Information card shows your employee profile:

  • Full name, Employee ID, Designation, Department
  • Email, Office email, Mobile, Office phone, CNIC
  • Date of joining, Shift timings
This section is view only. To update any information, contact your administrator.

Change Password

1

Open your profile menu

Click your name or avatar in the top navigation bar.

2

Enter current and new password

Type your current password, then your new password (minimum 8 characters).

3

Save

Your password is updated immediately. The admin can see the new password in the system if needed.
